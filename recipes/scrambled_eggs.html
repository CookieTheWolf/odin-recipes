Scrambled eggs

Description

Quick and easy to make healthy breakfast

Ingredients

  • Eggs (3-4)
  • 1 onion
  • 3 slices of your choice ham
  • Chives

Directions

  1. Chopp your onion and cut your ham in to small squares and fry till golden brown
  2. break and whisk your eggs with salt and freshly grinded pepper and pour them on pan
  3. do not stop stiring
  4. when the eggs are allset tahe them out the fire and put on plate
  5. sprinkle your scrambled eggs with chives
  6. Enjoy!
