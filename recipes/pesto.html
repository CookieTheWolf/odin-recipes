Pesto

Description

My favourite way of serving pasta

With green basil or red tomato and pepper pesto and sprinkled with motzarella chese

Ingredients

  • 300g noodles of ypur choice
  • 1/2 of pesto jar (green or red, its up to you)
  • motzarella chese
  • Basil leafs

Directions

  1. boil your noodles till aldente
  2. pour 30ml of noodle water in to the cup and save for later
  3. take your noodles out and put them in to the empty pot
  4. put 1/2 of pesto jar in to the noodles and pour in the noodle water
  5. stir untill all noodles are covered with pesto
  6. put your noodles on plate, sprinkle with chese and garnish with basil leafs
  7. Enjoy
